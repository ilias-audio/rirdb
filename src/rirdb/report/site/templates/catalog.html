How the counts are made. For datasets rirdb has indexed, IRs and spaces are counted in the index. An IR record is one source position heard at one receiver position: the channels of a binaural or Ambisonic capture count once, while dense single-microphone grids count every microphone. A space is a room, hall or outdoor site as its dataset names it.{% if shown_published %} Where the index holds only part of a release, the published count is shown instead ({{ shown_published | join(", ") }}).{% endif %} For the other datasets the counts are those the authors publish; {{ (counts.all - counts.irs_stated) | int }} datasets publish no IR count, shown as a dash. Sample rates are those found in the files for indexed datasets, otherwise those published.

Availability groups the download route: open download; registration, request or manual download; or a broken link or an archived copy only. rirdb: analysed datasets have per-IR quality checks and acoustic parameters. Registry datasets that are not analysed are fetched but not yet indexed, simulated or deferred, or need a manual or registered download; each of their pages says which. Surveyed datasets are catalogued from their web pages only.{% if twins %} Surveyed records that describe a dataset the registry also holds, in whole or in part, link to its registry entry and are counted in both groups ({{ twins | join(", ") }}).{% endif %}

Captured as groups capture formats into five families: mono, stereo, binaural, Ambisonic or spherical array (including raw Eigenmike and tetrahedral capsules), and other microphone arrays.
