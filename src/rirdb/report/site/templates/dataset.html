Where it sits on the CLAP map

The CLAP map places IRs by how a pretrained audio model ({{ map.model }}) hears them: once for the IR itself, and once for what it does to dry recordings. Grey: the corpus ({{ map.n_map | int }} IRs, at most {{ map.cap | int }} per dataset). Blue: this dataset. Distances and scores use the full embedding; the flat picture is only a guide.

{% for s in map.signals %} {% endfor %}

Realism is the share of this dataset's clean room IRs that fall inside the neighbourhood of at least one clean measured room IR from another dataset. Coverage is the share of the other datasets' clean room IRs (and of their rooms) that have at least one IR of this dataset inside their neighbourhood. A neighbourhood is the distance from a reference IR to its {{ map.k | int }}th-nearest neighbour from other datasets, since CLAP groups IRs by their recording chain.
