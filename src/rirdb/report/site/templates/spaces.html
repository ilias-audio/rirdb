Medians of clean IRs

T30, EDT, C80 and DRR are medians over the space's clean measured IRs: one representation per position, duplicates removed, and no IR flagged as a sweep, tonal, dropping out or not decaying. T30 and EDT are means of the 500 Hz and 1 kHz octaves. Where fewer than half of those IRs allow a T30, the T30-or-T20 median is shown and marked T20 ({{ sp.t20 | int }} spaces). A dash means no clean IR gave a value.{% if sp.subsampled %} For {{ sp.subsampled | join(", ") }}, only a fixed subsample of the IRs was analysed, so clean IRs can be far fewer than IRs.{% endif %}

Corrected categories

Categories come from keyword rules on the space names. The rules misread some names ("field" in Huddersfield, "street" in North Street), leave single-room laboratory datasets unlabelled, and cannot tell that a library was modelled rather than recorded. The {{ sp.fixed | int }} spaces corrected by hand are marked corrected; hover the mark for the reason.

One room, several datasets

Some rooms were measured for more than one dataset, or for several measurement lines of one dataset (the ACE rooms, the Arni room). The table keeps one row per indexed space, {{ sp.n | int }} in all, and links each repeat to its first entry; counted once, they are {{ sp.physical | int }} physical rooms and sites. The physical_id column in spaces.csv holds the link.
